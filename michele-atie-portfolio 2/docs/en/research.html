Scientific work

My doctoral research focuses on the sensory experience of atmospheres, explored across seven architectural sites designed by architects including Le Corbusier, Jean Nouvel, SANAA, Auguste Perret, Henri Labrouste and Tadao Ando.

I developed a questionnaire and an evaluation method and conducted experiments. This work contributes to discussions on architectural design and education.

This research has been shared through publications and presentations, including at IEEE VR, the CIE and ACM Multimedia. I also contributed to a multidisciplinary ANR-funded research project within the AAU-CRENAU laboratory.
